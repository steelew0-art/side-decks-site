QUESTIONS

Good to know before we start.

Don’t see your question? Send us a project inquiry and include whatever you already know.

What should I send with my project inquiry?+

Anything that helps explain the space: photos of the house and yard, rough dimensions, a hand sketch, an existing plan, inspiration images or notes about how you want to use the deck.

Do I need a finished design before contacting Side Decks?+

No. You can start with a rough idea. The inquiry form is designed for homeowners who have either a clear plan or just a starting point.

What areas do you serve?+

Our primary service area is Pender, New Hanover and Brunswick counties in coastal southeastern North Carolina. Send your address with your inquiry so we can confirm your project area.

Can you work with an existing deck or porch?+

Yes, deck replacement, rebuild and repair projects are part of the project types we consider. Photos and a description of the existing structure are especially helpful.

Can I upload sketches or inspiration photos?+

Yes. The project form accepts JPG, PNG, WEBP and PDF files, with up to eight files at 10 MB each.

How far in advance should I contact you?+

Tell us your anticipated timeline even if it is flexible. That gives us useful context when we follow up about your project.
